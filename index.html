    Chapter 3The House on Hopland

    Reviewers describe Luddites floor by floor. Tap a floor on the drawing.

    Second floor · Residents only

    The flat upstairs

    The rooms above the shop have never stood empty. Richard and Katia lived here first, with Plushkin and Catsby. Then you and Stijn, with Jip. Now it's Catherine, long since part of the furniture, with Molly, a guitar, a keyboard, a singing voice and Harry Potter on the PlayStation.

    Residents so far: three cats, one dachshund, five humans, one guitar, one keyboard. And, down in the basement, spiders beyond counting.
    First floor & terrace

    The wine bar

    Wine, coffee, cookies, cheese and a sun-trap terrace. No wifi, on purpose. Reviewers call it “cosy” more than anything else.

    The wine bar upstairs has a great menu and you can tell the whole operation is run with love and care.

    179 mention wine · 56 coffee & tea · 36 the terrace
    Ground floor

    The bookshop

    Wooden shelves to the ceiling, ladders, chandeliers, special editions and handwritten staff picks. Famous for the best English selection in Belgium.

    Walking through this store is like walking through a fey portal and forgetting time and space for a moment.

    128 praise the selection · 72 mention English books · 82 call it beautiful
    Basement

    Children's & YA

    The secret downstairs that visitors love to tell each other about. Also a known hideout for readers when the bar gets lively.

    If you visit, be sure to take the stairs down to the YA/children's section.

    Chapter 7Marginalia

    A handful of notes in pencil. Out of 657 reviews, these are the only recurring wishes.

    It's gotten popular 13 reviews

    Busy weekends, a lively bar, and seats that are hard to find after 2 pm. A few readers miss the quiet.

    Readers already know the basement is the quiet escape.

    The gift voucher fine print 1 review

    One visitor didn't know the voucher only works downstairs. It isn't printed on the card.

    One extra line on the voucher would fix it.

    A gluten-free nibble 1 wish

    “The only thing that would make it even better is if there would be at least one gluten free option.”

    Someone else already loved the GF matcha cake, so it has been done.

    Stairs & budgets a few reviews

    The bar is upstairs, which is hard for some. A couple of people find the wine a bit pricey (“about €7 a glass, not really expensive either”).

    The half-price-with-a-book deal is the best answer to this one.

    Only one of the 657 reviews has an owner reply so far. Plenty of lovely people out there who'd be thrilled to hear back from the Luddites.

    October 2018

    One dark and stormy night

    In the shop's own words, Luddites was “dreamed up by Richard Bolte and Jorien Caers on one dark, stormy night”, followed by months of planning, renovating and swearing off paint cans for good.

    Epilogue

    Liefje,

    Six and a half years ago you and Richard opened a door on Hopland and invited the city into a hideout from the modern world. Since then 657 strangers have gone home and written about it, and when you read them all in one sitting a pattern appears. They don't just praise the books or the wine. They talk about how it felt: welcome, warm, unhurried, a little bit enchanted.

    “Friendly”, “kind” or “welcoming” turn up in 64 reviews. “Cosy” in 57. Somebody wrote that they want to live there, and somebody else that the place made them want to open their own. Those words are about you, and Richard, and Isa, and the whole team.

    I'm so proud of you. (And a belated happy birthday to Richard. The 4.9 is your present too.)

    Stijn
    Be there or be haunted by regret.
